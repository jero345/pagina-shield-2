When our help is worth having

Some complaints are not straightforward

These are the situations where a lawyer is most likely to change the outcome for Shield investors. If one of them applies to you, ask us for a free assessment.

01

Macquarie repaid your capital

You may still be owed the returns you would have earned in your old fund. AFCA has awarded these even after Macquarie's repayment.

02

You invested through Equity Trustees

Members of AMG Super and Super Simplifier have not been repaid. ASIC is suing the trustee, but that case will not be heard before 2027.

03

Your adviser was with MWL

MWL Financial Services is in liquidation. If AFCA decides in your favour, the Compensation Scheme of Last Resort can pay, up to $150,000.

04

You were advised through InterPrac

AFCA has paused decisions on InterPrac complaints while InterPrac's court case against AFCA runs. We keep your complaint on track.

05

More than one adviser or platform

Every responsible firm needs to be identified and included in your complaint, or you may leave compensation behind.

06

You used an SMSF

Your fund is the complainant, and the cost of winding up the SMSF can form part of the claim.

07

Your loss is over $150,000, or you lost insurance

The Scheme's cap is $150,000, and life, TPD or trauma cover lost on the switch can be part of the claim. We look at what else can be recovered.

08

You are in a class action or offered a deed

We check how the Macquarie class action affects your complaint, and whether any deed or settlement asks you to give up other rights.

Where things stand

What has happened with Shield

February 2024

ASIC stops new investment

ASIC made interim stop orders halting new offers of Shield.

June to August 2024

Assets frozen, receivers appointed

The Federal Court froze Shield's assets and appointed receivers. Keystone has since gone into liquidation.

August 2025

ASIC sues Equity Trustees

ASIC is suing Equity Trustees over about $160 million invested in Shield, seeking compensation for members.

September 2025

Macquarie repays capital

Macquarie repaid about $321 million invested in Shield by its members, less withdrawals.

June 2026

ASIC sues Keystone's directors

ASIC alleges about $305 million was moved to related entities without proper safeguards.

Now

AFCA and the Scheme

AFCA is deciding complaints against most advisers, but InterPrac complaints are paused until at least February 2027, and the Scheme is waiting for funding.

About our fees

What you are paying for

AFCA and the Compensation Scheme of Last Resort do not charge you. Our fee is for our work on your complaint, not for access to AFCA.

Where a complaint is complex, we will ask AFCA to order the financial firm to contribute to your legal costs. AFCA usually caps a contribution at $5,000 and does not always make one. Any contribution reduces what you pay us.

We only take on a complaint where the likely compensation is well above our fee. Before you sign anything we will give you a written costs agreement and disclosure explaining the fee, when it is payable and your rights.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a lawyer to complain to AFCA?

No. AFCA is free and designed to be used without a lawyer. We act only where your circumstances make our involvement worthwhile, and we will tell you if they do not.

Macquarie repaid my money. Do I still have a claim?

Possibly. AFCA has ordered advisers to pay the returns investors would have earned in their previous fund, after allowing for Macquarie's repayment. The 1 July 2027 date matters for these claims.

I invested through Equity Trustees and have not been repaid.

You can complain to AFCA about your adviser now. ASIC's case against Equity Trustees seeks compensation for members, but it will not be heard before 2027.

My adviser was with MWL. What happens?

MWL is in liquidation. If AFCA decides in your favour and MWL cannot pay, you can apply to the Compensation Scheme of Last Resort, which pays up to $150,000 including interest.

My adviser was with InterPrac. What now?

AFCA is still accepting and investigating InterPrac complaints, but will not make decisions until InterPrac's case against AFCA ends. Make your complaint now so it is in the queue.

How much will it cost me?

Nothing upfront, and nothing if your complaint does not succeed. If you receive compensation, our fixed fee is $5,000 including GST, paid from that compensation. AFCA itself is free.
